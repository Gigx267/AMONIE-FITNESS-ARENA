Our Story

Amonie Fitness Arena began as a small fitness studio with a big vision: to create a community-focused gym that empowers individuals to transform their lives through fitness.

What started as a modest facility has grown into Idu's premier fitness destination, serving hundreds of members with state-of-the-art equipment, diverse programs, and expert guidance.
